# Reading list

Books and papers by topic, matching the parts of the ladder. Free resources are marked **(free)**. Titles and authors are
given so you can find the current edition or the open-access version; check the link of any online resource before
relying on it.

## General signal processing (all parts)

* Smith, S.W. *The Scientist and Engineer's Guide to Digital Signal Processing*. **(free at dspguide.com)**; the gentlest start.
* Lyons, R.G. *Understanding Digital Signal Processing*; intuitive, practical, many worked examples.
* Oppenheim, A.V., Willsky, A.S. *Signals and Systems*; the standard text for Parts A and B.
* Oppenheim, A.V., Schafer, R.W. *Discrete-Time Signal Processing*; the reference for Parts B and D.
* Proakis, J.G., Manolakis, D.G. *Digital Signal Processing*.
* Orfanidis, S.J. *Introduction to Signal Processing*. **(free online from the author)**

## Part A: sensors, noise, sampling (problems 01–02)

* Allan, D.W. (1966). "Statistics of atomic frequency standards." Proc. IEEE 54, 221.
* El-Sheimy, N., Hou, H., Niu, X. (2008). "Analysis and modeling of inertial sensors using Allan variance." IEEE Trans. Instrum. Meas. 57, 140.
* Shannon, C.E. (1949). "Communication in the presence of noise." Proc. IRE 37, 10.
* Vaughan, R.G., Scott, N.L., White, D.R. (1991). "The theory of bandpass sampling." IEEE Trans. Signal Process. 39, 1973.
* Kester, W. (ed.) *Data Conversion Handbook*, Analog Devices. **(free)**

## Part B: mathematics of signals and systems (problems 03–07)

* Lathi, B.P. *Linear Systems and Signals*.
* Bracewell, R.N. *The Fourier Transform and Its Applications*.
* Nise, N.S. *Control Systems Engineering* (Laplace transform, stability, Bode).
* Franklin, G.F., Powell, J.D., Workman, M.L. *Digital Control of Dynamic Systems* (discretisation, sample-rate effects).
* Papoulis, A., Pillai, S.U. *Probability, Random Variables and Stochastic Processes*.
* Kaplan, E.D., Hegarty, C.J. (eds.) *Understanding GPS/GNSS: Principles and Applications* (code ranging).
* Li, T.-P., Ma, Y.-Q. (1983). "Analysis methods for results in gamma-ray astronomy." Astrophys. J. 272, 317.

## Part C: electronics for signal processing (problems 08–11)

* Horowitz, P., Hill, W. *The Art of Electronics*; filters, noise, op-amps, converters.
* Sedra, A.S., Smith, K.C. *Microelectronic Circuits*.
* Schaumann, R., Van Valkenburg, M.E. *Design of Analog Filters*.
* Motchenbacher, C.D., Connelly, J.A. *Low-Noise Electronic System Design*.
* Schreier, R., Temes, G.C. *Understanding Delta-Sigma Data Converters*.
* Sklar, B. *Digital Communications: Fundamentals and Applications*.
* Larson, W.J., Wertz, J.R. (eds.) *Space Mission Analysis and Design* (link budgets).
* Gardner, F.M. *Phaselock Techniques*.
* Analog Devices application notes and tutorials on op-amp noise, active filters and converters. **(free)**

## Part D: digital signal processing (problems 12–20)

* Harris, F.J. (1978). "On the use of windows for harmonic analysis with the discrete Fourier transform." Proc. IEEE 66, 51.
* Cooley, J.W., Tukey, J.W. (1965). "An algorithm for the machine calculation of complex Fourier series." Math. Comput. 19, 297.
* Welch, P.D. (1967). "The use of fast Fourier transform for the estimation of power spectra." IEEE Trans. Audio Electroacoust. 15, 70.
* Thomson, D.J. (1982). "Spectrum estimation and harmonic analysis." Proc. IEEE 70, 1055.
* Stoica, P., Moses, R. *Spectral Analysis of Signals*.
* VanderPlas, J.T. (2018). "Understanding the Lomb–Scargle periodogram." Astrophys. J. Suppl. 236, 16 (arXiv:1703.09824).
* Parks, T.W., McClellan, J.H. (1972). "Chebyshev approximation for nonrecursive digital filters with linear phase." IEEE Trans. Circuit Theory 19, 189.
* Crochiere, R.E., Rabiner, L.R. *Multirate Digital Signal Processing*.
* Mallat, S. *A Wavelet Tour of Signal Processing*.
* Torrence, C., Compo, G.P. (1998). "A practical guide to wavelet analysis." Bull. Am. Meteorol. Soc. 79, 61.
* Savitzky, A., Golay, M.J.E. (1964). "Smoothing and differentiation of data by simplified least squares procedures." Anal. Chem. 36, 1627.
* Donoho, D.L., Johnstone, I.M. (1994). "Ideal spatial adaptation by wavelet shrinkage." Biometrika 81, 425.
* Kay, S.M. *Fundamentals of Statistical Signal Processing*, Vol. I (estimation) and Vol. II (detection).
* Allen, B. et al. (2012). "FINDCHIRP: an algorithm for detection of gravitational waves from inspiraling compact binaries." Phys. Rev. D 85, 122006.
* Abbott, B.P. et al. (2016). "Observation of gravitational waves from a binary black hole merger." Phys. Rev. Lett. 116, 061102 (arXiv:1602.03837).
* Haykin, S. *Adaptive Filter Theory*.
* Kalman, R.E. (1960). "A new approach to linear filtering and prediction problems." J. Basic Eng. 82, 35.
* Labbe, R. *Kalman and Bayesian Filters in Python*. **(free on GitHub)**
* Bar-Shalom, Y., Li, X.R., Kirubarajan, T. *Estimation with Applications to Tracking and Navigation*.
* Groves, P.D. *Principles of GNSS, Inertial, and Multisensor Integrated Navigation Systems*.

## Part E: machine learning and the capstone (problems 21–25)

* Randall, R.B. *Vibration-Based Condition Monitoring*.
* Smith, W.A., Randall, R.B. (2015). "Rolling element bearing diagnostics using the Case Western Reserve University data: a benchmark study." Mech. Syst. Signal Process. 64–65, 100.
* Hastie, T., Tibshirani, R., Friedman, J. *The Elements of Statistical Learning*. **(free from the authors)**
* Géron, A. *Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras and TensorFlow*.
* Ester, M. et al. (1996). "A density-based algorithm for discovering clusters in large spatial databases with noise." Proc. KDD-96.
* Liu, F.T., Ting, K.M., Zhou, Z.-H. (2008). "Isolation forest." Proc. IEEE ICDM.
* Chandola, V., Banerjee, A., Kumar, V. (2009). "Anomaly detection: a survey." ACM Computing Surveys 41, 15.
* Goodfellow, I., Bengio, Y., Courville, A. *Deep Learning*. **(free online)**
* Ismail Fawaz, H. et al. (2019). "Deep learning for time series classification: a review." Data Min. Knowl. Discov. 33, 917 (arXiv:1809.04356).
* Zevin, M. et al. (2017). "Gravity Spy: integrating Advanced LIGO detector characterization, machine learning, and citizen science." Class. Quantum Grav. 34, 064003.
* Hundman, K. et al. (2018). "Detecting spacecraft anomalies using LSTMs and nonparametric dynamic thresholding." Proc. KDD (arXiv:1802.04431).
* Wilson, G. et al. (2014). "Best practices for scientific computing." PLoS Biology 12, e1001745.
* Wilson, G. et al. (2017). "Good enough practices in scientific computing." PLoS Comput. Biol. 13, e1005510.
